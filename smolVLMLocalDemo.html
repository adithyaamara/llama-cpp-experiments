🛠️ Setup Requirements

1. Run a vision model locally with llama.cpp server: llama-server -hf ggml-org/SmolVLM2-500M-Video-Instruct-GGUF -n 100
2. Enable camera permissions in your browser
3. Make sure your browser supports WebRTC and getUserMedia APIs
